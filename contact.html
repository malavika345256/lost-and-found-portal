Frequently Asked Questions

How do I report a lost item?

Go to the Report Lost page and provide the item details, location, date and your contact information.

How do I report an item I found?

Select Report Found from the navigation menu and submit the details of the item you found.

How can I search for my lost item?

Use the Search Items page to search submitted Lost and Found reports by item name, category and type.

How can I check my submitted reports?

Open the My Reports page to view the reports stored through the portal.
